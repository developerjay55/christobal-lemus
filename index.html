About

The Player
Behind #16

Christobal Yadhir Lemus Pinto — known everywhere as Chris — is a 9th grader at Largo High School who already plays like an upperclassman. A true Swiss army knife on the pitch, Chris commands four positions across both flanks and the center of the park, giving coaches rare tactical flexibility in a freshman.

His game is built on intelligence, versatility, and relentless work rate. Whether he's locking down the left flank as a fullback, winning balls in the midfield engine room, stepping in as a composed center back, or driving forward as a left winger — Chris brings the same intensity to every role.

Off the pitch, he carries that same discipline into the classroom: a 3.6 GPA, Honors coursework across Science, English, and World History, and Honor Roll recognition. The vision is clear — earn a D1 scholarship, then build a career in law.
